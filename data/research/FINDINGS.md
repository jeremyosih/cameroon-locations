## 1. Akoeman: department and possible transfer

**Finding — keep Nyong-et-So’o. Confidence: high for legal placement; medium-high for the explanation of BUCREP’s conflicting placement.** The signed scan of [decree 2010/198, 16 June 2010](https://s1084e755aa436055.jimcontent.com/download/version/1592993857/module/10895481257/name/Decret_ErectionDistricts%20en%20Arrond_160610.pdf), PDF p. 4, places “Arrondissement d’AKOEMAN, chef-lieu : Akoeman” under the Nyong-et-So’o heading. Its territory is that of the former Akoeman district, with Mbalmayo’s territory modified. This is direct administrative evidence, not a boundary-overlap inference. [decree 95/082, 24 April 1995](https://s1084e755aa436055.jimcontent.com/download/version/1583652904/module/12263713557/name/D%C3%A9cret%20no%2095_082%20du%2024%20avril%201995%20portant%20cr%C3%A9ation%20de%20communes%20rurales.pdf), PDF p. 3, already places the Akoeman rural commune under Nyong-et-So’o and ties it to the Akoeman district. [MINFI budget nomenclature, administrative table pp. 27–39](https://minfi.gov.cm/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/INTEGRAL_Nomenclature_Budgetaire_de_l_Etat_ULTIMATE.pdf), p. 30, independently groups code 1190 Akoeman under 119 Nyong et So’o.

BUCREP’s conflicting parent is present in the printed document: Akoeman appears as section 2.9.1 under Nyong-et-Mfoumou, PDF p. 88 / printed p. 87. It is not solely an extraction mistake. The same publication explains in its front matter that its administrative presentation incorporates units created after field collection. [[BUCREP Répertoire, complete INS copy](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Repertoire_actualise_villages_cameroun1-3.pdf)]

**Transfer decree: not found.** The 1995 and 2010 texts support an editorial misclassification more strongly than a move between departments. A temporary intervening transfer cannot be disproved merely from those two dates. **Action:** keep `CM002010001 → CM002010`; retain the BUCREP parent verbatim as a documented source conflict, with an inferred-editorial-error annotation rather than a fictional transfer date. [[decree 95/082, 24 April 1995](https://s1084e755aa436055.jimcontent.com/download/version/1583652904/module/12263713557/name/D%C3%A9cret%20no%2095_082%20du%2024%20avril%201995%20portant%20cr%C3%A9ation%20de%20communes%20rurales.pdf); [decree 2010/198, 16 June 2010](https://s1084e755aa436055.jimcontent.com/download/version/1592993857/module/10895481257/name/Decret_ErectionDistricts%20en%20Arrond_160610.pdf)]

## 2. Kentzou, Bombé, Nguelebok, Ndem-Nam and Kentzou II

**Kentzou → Bombé: confirmed, high confidence.** The decree states “Arrondissement de BOMBE, chef-lieu : Kentzou” (PDF p. 6), with the former Bombé district as its territory and Ndélélé’s limits modified. **Nguelebok → Ndem-Nam: confirmed, high confidence.** It states “Arrondissement de NDEM-NAM, chef-lieu : Nguelebok” (p. 7), with the former Ndem-Nam district as its territory and Batouri’s limits modified. Keep `CM003003002` and `CM003003007` respectively. [[decree 2010/198, 16 June 2010](https://s1084e755aa436055.jimcontent.com/download/version/1592993857/module/10895481257/name/Decret_ErectionDistricts%20en%20Arrond_160610.pdf)]

BUCREP separately lists **KENTZOU II, 228 residents (104 male, 124 female)** under Ndélélé, PDF p. 125 / printed p. 124. A later communal-forest management plan also lists Kentzou II in its table of Ndélélé’s villages: table 10, “Répartition ethnique de la Commune de Ndélélé.” The latter was accessible through indexed text; its direct PDF request returned 403. These independent listings support treating Kentzou II as a separate village record, not automatically moving it into Bombé. [[BUCREP Répertoire, complete INS copy](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Repertoire_actualise_villages_cameroun1-3.pdf); [Ndélélé forest management plan 2023](https://www.foretcommunale-cameroun.org/Texteslegaux_doc_cartes_FC_Cameroun/FC_Ndelele/PA_FC_NDELELE_2023.pdf)]

**Exact location and relationship to Kentzou town: not found conclusively.** Jeremy’s local identification would help distinguish a separate settlement, an outlying quarter or a historical naming relationship. **Action:** keep the village under Ndélélé pending location evidence; do not use name resemblance to override its recorded parent. The arrondissement mappings do not depend on resolving this village question. [[decree 2010/198, 16 June 2010](https://s1084e755aa436055.jimcontent.com/download/version/1592993857/module/10895481257/name/Decret_ErectionDistricts%20en%20Arrond_160610.pdf); [BUCREP Répertoire, complete INS copy](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Repertoire_actualise_villages_cameroun1-3.pdf)]

## 8. Wikidata P-code mapping and useful enrichment

[wikidata-pcodes.json](wikidata-pcodes.json) contains all **428 input P-codes**: **10 regions and 58 departments matched**, **349 ADM3 candidates**, and **11 ADM3 not found** in the executed queries. These are research matches, not an assertion that every candidate represents a distinct arrondissement. All matched rows link to their Wikidata entity and retain type, match method, parent-check status and import recommendation. The raw endpoint responses, query text and counts are saved in [wikidata-query-metadata.json](wikidata-query-metadata.json). Source: [Wikidata SPARQL endpoint](https://query.wikidata.org/sparql), queries reproduced alongside this report.

**Entity-type distinction is material:** of the 349 ADM3 candidates, **3** are explicitly typed as [arrondissement of Cameroon, Q106698210](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q106698210), **332** are [commune of Cameroon, Q3076994](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3076994) items, sometimes also representing towns, and **14** are locality/generic administrative candidates requiring scope review. The three explicitly typed arrondissements are [Bafoussam I](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q106698066), [Bafoussam II](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q106698080), and [Bafoussam III](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q106698052). Filtering only the arrondissement class therefore misses most potentially useful commune records. [Query files: `wikidata-query.sparql`, `wikidata-extra-query.sparql`; endpoint](https://query.wikidata.org/sparql).

**OCHA P-codes are supported by Wikidata:** [P14503 is OCHA Place Code](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P14503). The query selecting values beginning `CM` returned **zero bindings**, so no mapping here is an exact OCHA-property match. [P1566](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P1566) is GeoNames ID, [P8119](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P8119) is HASC, and [P10581](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P10581) is Cameroun COG. The inspected matched items returned no HASC values; consequently the delivered matches actually use normalized labels/aliases and parent/type checks, not HASC joins. COG and OCHA codes must not be treated as interchangeable. [Saved property-discovery and P-code queries; endpoint](https://query.wikidata.org/sparql).

The enrichment export contains available values for **414 coordinate-bearing matches, 71 capital-bearing matches, 43 website-bearing matches, 263 GeoNames-bearing matches and 18 matches with population statements explicitly dated in 2005**. These counts cover all matched levels. Coordinates may locate a settlement rather than the administrative centroid; a 2005 date alone does not prove population extent matches the BUCREP unit. Keep reference URLs/stated-in qualifiers and review discrepancies before importing. The source for each candidate is embedded in [wikidata-enrichment.json](wikidata-enrichment.json); properties: [P625](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P625), [P36](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P36), [P856](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P856), [P1082](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P1082), [P585](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P585).

The **11 not-found ADM3 items** are Ngaoundéré II, Bertoua I and II, Garoua I–III, Ebolowa I and II, and Kumba I–III. A city-wide QID was not substituted for these subdivisions. A false homonym, [Buéa the motor ship](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1018402), was explicitly excluded; [Buéa the Cameroon locality](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q312209) remains a locality/administrative candidate, not a certified distinct arrondissement item. These negative results concern the executed label/class queries; they do not establish that no suitable Wikidata item exists. [Endpoint and saved query set](https://query.wikidata.org/sparql).

**Action:** import regions/departments only after normal review; stage ADM3 matches as candidates, with entity scope visible. Add commune websites and chef-lieux where references support the same unit. Keep authoritative population and boundary provenance separate. Wikidata structured data is offered under [CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing); Wikipedia prose has separate [reuse terms](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use).
